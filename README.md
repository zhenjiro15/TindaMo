# 🚀 Tinda Mo V1.0.0 – Initial Release

Introducing **Tinda Mo V1.0.0!**

Welcome to the very first official release of **Tinda Mo**, an application designed to help store owners manage their business more easily and efficiently.

This is the beginning of a smarter way to manage your store, track sales, organize inventory, and monitor customer debts.

## ✨ What's Included

- Store and account management.
- Item and inventory management.
- Cashier and sales system.
- Customer debt tracking.
- Sales records and transaction history.
- Simple and user-friendly interface.

## 📦 Release Information

- **App:** Tinda Mo
- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Release Type:** Initial Official Release
- **Platform:** Android & Website
